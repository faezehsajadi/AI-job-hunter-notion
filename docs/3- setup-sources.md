# Setup — Sources (Quick)

This workflow pulls jobs from:
- Gmail job alerts (including LinkedIn alerts)
- Indeed connector
- ZipRecruiter connector

---

## 1) Gmail (job alert inbox hygiene)
- Confirm job alert emails are arriving from:
  - `linkedin.com`
  - `indeed.com`
  - `ziprecruiter.com`
- (Optional) Create a Gmail label, e.g. `Job Alerts`
- (Optional) Add a Gmail filter to auto-label these emails
- Make sure alerts are not going to **Spam** or getting buried in **Promotions**
  - If needed, move one alert email to Inbox to “train” Gmail

---

## 2) LinkedIn (via Gmail alerts)
- Turn on LinkedIn job alerts for your target roles/keywords
- Prefer alerts that include direct job links
- Keep saved searches aligned with the skill categories:
  - InfoSec, Cybersecurity, Cryptography, Post‑Quantum, Smart Contract Dev/Audit

---

## 3) Indeed
- Create saved searches for your target keywords
- Prefer Remote/Hybrid filters (based on your preference)
- (Optional) Enable email alerts as a backup channel

---

## 4) ZipRecruiter
- Create saved searches for your target keywords
- Prefer remote-friendly filters where possible
- (Optional) Enable email alerts as a backup

---

## 5) Keyword hygiene (recommended)
- Keep 5–15 strong keywords (avoid huge noisy lists)
- Use role titles + hard skills (examples: `cryptography engineer`, `post quantum cryptography`, `smart contract auditor`)
- Review monthly and remove low-signal terms
- Keywords are user-specific — choose your own roles/skills and iterate until results are high-signal

---

## Verification
- Run the skill once.
- If results are noisy: tighten saved searches/keywords.
- If results are empty: broaden keywords or check Gmail alert delivery.
